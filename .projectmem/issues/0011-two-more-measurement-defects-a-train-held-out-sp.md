# #0011 Two more measurement defects. (a) Train/held-out splits detect overfitting of FITTED PARAMETERS; hand-written rules have none, so the ML vocabulary implies a rigor that is not present and reads as a tell. Reframe as pre-registration and hold-out discipline: the training split is the only data looked at while tuning, the held-out split runs exactly ONCE at the end, and the README states the split was frozen before tuning. (b) False-positive cost in rupees is not defensible as 'the order total that would have been real revenue': the totals come from the builder's own seeded price distribution, blocked GMV is not lost revenue when the graceful-failure story has the agent re-plan and substitute, and GMV/merchant margin/Razorpay MDR are three different numbers. Restate as blocked benign GMV at the seeded price distribution, explicitly an upper bound, alongside a recovery rate and a net figure, and publish the price distribution so a reader can rescale.
