# #0021 THE FALSE-POSITIVE COST METRIC HAS NO DATA, and it is the metric IDEA.md calls the differentiator. DUK-19's report on the train split prints 'Blocked benign GMV, UPPER BOUND: 0.00 rupees' because 84 of 84 benign transcripts were ALLOWED — zero false positives, so nothing was blocked, so the cost figure is trivially zero and says nothing. Recovery rate is correctly reported NOT MEASURABLE and net NOT COMPUTABLE, both honest, but they are downstream of the same emptiness. This matters because IDEA.md's cross-track argument rests entirely on this number: 'a false positive in the gate is a legitimate purchase blocked, i.e. merchant revenue lost, so false-positive cost denominated in rupees of blocked legitimate GMV is simultaneously the Track 02 honesty metric and the Track 01 GROWTH metric. One number closing both halves.' That number is currently 0.00 with n=0, which is not a measurement. ROOT CAUSE, almost certainly the benign generator rather than the gate: src/eval/benign.ts produces 'ordinary shopping within policy', i.e. baskets comfortably inside every threshold, so the gate never has an opportunity to be wrong. A false-positive rate measured over a population engineered to contain no near-misses is guaranteed to be zero and proves nothing. FIX, and it belongs before DUK-20 scores the held-out split once: add near-boundary benign sessions to the benign corpus — legitimate shopping that sits just under a cap or just under the approval threshold, plus genuinely ambiguous-but-allowed categories (the seed data already has the Vim-dishwash-versus-Lifebuoy-handwash ambiguity for exactly this purpose). Those are the sessions where an over-tight rule would wrongly block, and they are the only sessions that can give the cost figure a non-zero denominator. Without them the report should state plainly that the false-positive cost is unmeasured rather than zero, on the same principle that made recovery rate NOT MEASURABLE instead of 0%.
